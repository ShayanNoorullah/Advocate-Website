Questions

Before you reach out.

Something else on your mind? Ask on WhatsApp or call +92 333 2522517.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the booking form below — choose your matter, a preferred date and how you would like to meet. Your request opens in WhatsApp or email, ready to send, and the firm confirms your appointment.

Can the consultation happen by phone or video?

Yes. Consultations are available in person at the Hyderabad or Karachi office, by phone, or online by video call.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Every enquiry is treated as strictly confidential from the very first contact. The website itself stores nothing — your request goes directly to the advocate.

Which courts does the firm appear before?

The firm represents clients before courts of first instance through to the Honourable High Court, as well as tribunals and special courts.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Any documents connected to the matter — notices, orders, agreements, correspondence and your CNIC. If a hearing date is coming up, mention it when booking so it can be prioritised.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the nature and stage of the matter. They are discussed openly and agreed with you before any engagement begins.
